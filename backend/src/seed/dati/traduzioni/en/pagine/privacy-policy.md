Information pursuant to articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) for visitors to this website.

## Data controller

**SONOTRON ITALIA S.R.L.S.**, Strada Nazionale Adriatica Sud 44, 61032 Fano (PS), Italy. VAT and tax code 02654710413, REA 199122. Certified email: [sonotron@legalmail.it](mailto:sonotron@legalmail.it), email: [info@sonotronitalia.com](mailto:info@sonotronitalia.com).

## What data we process

- **Browsing data**: the systems that run the website record, as happens on every website, technical data such as the IP address, the browser used and the pages requested. They are used only to keep the website working and secure.
- **Data you send us**: if you write to us by email or with a form on the website, we process the data you provide (for example name, email, phone number, content of the message) to reply to you.
- **Cookies and similar tools**: see the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy).

## Why we process it and on what basis

- Running and protecting the website: legitimate interest of the controller (art. 6.1.f GDPR).
- Replying to your requests: steps taken at your request (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
- Visit statistics (Google Analytics) and measurement of advertising campaigns (Google Ads): only with your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time from "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of the page.

## How long we keep it

Browsing data for the time needed for the security of the website and in any case no longer than 12 months. Data from requests for the time needed to handle them and in any case no longer than 24 months, unless the law requires otherwise. Cookie choices for 6 months.

## Who we share it with

With the suppliers that help us run the website, appointed as data processors (for example the server provider, in Italy). With your consent, with Google (Google Ireland Ltd) for statistics and advertising: Google may transfer data to the United States, on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The data are not disclosed to the public.

## Your rights

You can request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing (articles 15-22 GDPR), by writing to the controller's contacts. You can also lodge a complaint with the [Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali)](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
