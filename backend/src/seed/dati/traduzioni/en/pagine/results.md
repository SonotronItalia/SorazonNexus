## Clinical review between 2003 and 2010

carried out at CENTRO MEDICO ARCADIA - FANO (PU)

Health Director Dr. GUALFARDO BARTOLUCCI

*MSc in Orthopaedic and Trauma medicine - Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation*

*former Head of Orthopaedic and Trauma medicine unit at Cagli (PU) Hospital*

**The following have undergone therapy:** No. 3199 men, No. 4136 women

**The following have been treated:**

- Non-weight-bearing joints (Shoulder - Elbow - Wrist - Hand)
- Weight-bearing joints (Hip - Knee - Foot - Ankle)
- Spine (C-spine - T-spine - L-spine - Sacrum)

Attested positive clinical results, based on assessment parameters: Pain - Movement

Effectiveness tested - 1/3/6 months follow-up
