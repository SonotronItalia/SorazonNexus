For everything else, see the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).

## How to manage your preferences

On your first visit a banner asks for your choice: you can accept, reject or choose by category. Closing the banner with the X rejects optional cookies. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page. Your choice lasts 6 months, after which we ask you again.

## Technical tools (always active)

They do not require consent because they are needed for the website to work. They are stored in your browser's memory and are not sent to anyone.

- **sorazon-consenso**: your cookie choices, 6 months
- **payload-theme**: the light or dark theme you chose
- **sorazon-effetti**: visual effects turned on or off

## Statistics (only with your consent)

- **Google Analytics 4** (Google Ireland Ltd): counting visits and page views, to improve the website. Cookies _ga and _ga_*, up to 2 years. If you withdraw your consent, we delete them from your browser.

## Marketing (only with your consent)

- **Google Ads** (Google Ireland Ltd): measuring the results of advertising campaigns and personalised ads. Google cookies for conversions and ad personalisation.

Google may transfer data to the United States, on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Information: [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## External content (only with your consent)

- **YouTube** (domain youtube-nocookie.com): videos embedded in the pages
- **Google Maps**: map on the Contact us page

Without consent this content is not loaded: in its place there is a button to load it once.
